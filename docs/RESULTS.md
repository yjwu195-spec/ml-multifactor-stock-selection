# Result catalog and provenance

`results/reference` was copied from the previous improved-v2 outputs during packaging on 2026-10-19. It was not regenerated by retraining. A SHA-256 inventory is in `release_manifest.json`.

| Artifact | Meaning |
|---|---|
| dataset_metadata_improved.json | Factor columns, split dates and row counts |
| validation_tuning_results.csv | Candidate parameters and train/validation/test scores |
| validation_selected_params.json | Selected settings and selection rule |
| extended_strategy_summary.csv / .json | Nine model-portfolio performance rows |
| extended_strategy_nav.csv | Period return, NAV and drawdown by model/portfolio |
| extended_strategy_period_returns.csv | Long/short/benchmark legs and costs |
| decile_backtest.csv | Ten-group gross returns, NAV and relative-return curves |
| decile_summary_checked.csv | Previously checked decile summaries; distinct from stock long-short |

## Figures and documents

The three files under `reports/figures` show archived Random Forest index-hedged, long-only and bottom-stock-short variants. Names containing `best`/`alternative`/`not_recommended` retain the earlier research labels, not present investment recommendations. Each image has NAV above and aligned drawdown below. They were copied without changing calculations.

`reports/research_report.docx` and `reports/strategy_roadshow.pptx` preserve prior presentation material. They predate the packaging audit. Their text/figures are not a substitute for the caveats in `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.

`reports/results_overview.html` is generated from archived tables by the stdlib-only `tools/build_results_report.py`. It deliberately labels results as historical and discloses the future-label filtering issue. New-run reports use `outputs/report.html`; reference tables are never overwritten by the main entry point.
