Machine Learning Multifactor Stock Selection

Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM; historical CSI 500 constituents; 20-trading-day rebalancing.

Historical research snapshot. The legacy backtest filters the investment universe by future-return labels. This is an ex-post selection bias: these results are not evidence of implementable out-of-sample performance. See docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md. This renderer does not run a backtest.

Methods and provenance

Source: Tushare Pro. Sample configuration: 2010-01-04 to 2025-04-30. Chronological 50/10/10/30 split. Boosting parameters selected by validation accuracy, then log loss and train-validation gap. Tables are copied from existing local outputs; no new full-sample run was performed during packaging.

Portfolio assumptions

Long Top 10; Long Top 10 / Short Bottom 10; Long Top 10 / Short CSI 500. Stock transaction cost: 10 bps per absolute weight turnover. Index short cost: 1 bp per rebalance period. Gross exposure of long-short portfolios is approximately 200% on the return-spread convention; financing and borrow constraints are not modeled.

Validation and remaining work

Use a full, label-independent investment universe, fit preprocessing without future information, purge overlapping label horizons, and rerun results. Period-frequency drawdown may understate intraperiod drawdown. Data licensing, point-in-time fundamentals, delisted-stock coverage and execution restrictions need further verification.
